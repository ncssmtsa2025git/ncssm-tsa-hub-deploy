# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
- **Members** (NCSSM Durham juniors and seniors in TSA): on a laptop in the residence hall late at night, or a phone between classes, checking their event's theme, rubric and deadlines, or logging in to submit check-ins. They need answers in seconds.
- **Visitors** (prospective students, parents, NCSSM staff, other chapters): land on the home page or gallery and decide what this chapter is about.

## Product Purpose
The chapter's home base: show who we are and what we've won, and give members one reliable place for this season's events, themes, rubrics, resources and team check-ins. Success means members stop asking "where's the rubric?" in the group chat, and visitors leave knowing NCSSM Durham TSA competes seriously.

## Brand Personality
Competitive, ambitious, curious. Confident without bragging; precise like a good engineering spec; warm because it's run by students for students. Copy is short, specific and active. No corporate filler ("empowering students through innovation").

## Anti-references
- Generic AI/SaaS templates: blue gradient hero with a centered line icon, identical icon-title-text card grids, vague mission statements.
- Corporate school websites buried in nav menus.
- Over-designed "editorial magazine" looks; this is a competition team, not a journal.

## Design Principles
1. **Show the chapter, not stock.** Real photos of our members at real conferences carry the identity.
2. **Answers first.** On reference pages (events, resources) the theme, team size, format and rubric are visible without hunting.
3. **Competition energy, engineering precision.** Bold condensed display type and confident color, paired with tidy, well-aligned information.
4. **Current or nothing.** Show only information confirmed for this season; label anything older.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast; full keyboard support (expandable rows, lightbox, filters); visible focus states; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; meaningful alt text on chapter photos.
